# 📤 Pinterest Manual Upload Guide (Step-by-Step)

This guide walks you through manually posting your approved Pins to Pinterest in under 60 seconds per Pin.

---

## ⚡ 60-Second Upload Workflow

For each Pin:

1. **Open Pinterest:** Go to [pinterest.com/pin-builder/](https://www.pinterest.com/pin-builder/) (or click **Create &rarr; Create Pin**).
2. **Upload Graphic:** Drag and drop the PNG from `pins/exports/` (Pins 01–30) or `growth-engine/ready-to-post/images/` (Pins 31–40).
3. **Paste Title:** Copy the **Title** from [`growth-engine/ready-to-post/csv/pinterest-final-upload-manifest.csv`](file:///Users/jake/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/whatnot-pinterest-landing/growth-engine/ready-to-post/csv/pinterest-final-upload-manifest.csv) or [`PINTEREST_FIRST_5.md`](file:///Users/jake/.gemini/antigravity-ide/scratch/whatnot-pinterest-landing/PINTEREST_FIRST_5.md).
4. **Paste Description:** Copy the **Description** (includes natural search keywords and tags).
5. **Select Board:** Choose the matching board from your 10 Pinterest boards.
6. **Add Destination Link:** Paste the specific landing page URL (with unique `utm_content` tag).
7. **Add Alt Text:** Click *Add alt text* and paste the description of the visual graphic.
8. **Publish or Schedule:** Click **Publish** (or select **Publish at a later date** using the 30-day plan).
9. **Mark as Uploaded:** Track progress in your publishing schedule.

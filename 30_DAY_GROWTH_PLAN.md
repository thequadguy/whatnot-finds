### Day 2: First Pin Wave (Pins 01 – 05)
- Open your local **Pin Template Studio** (`pins/index.html`) to export your first 5 pins.

## 📅 Phase 3: Iteration, A/B Testing & Scaling (Days 16–30)

### Day 16: Review Free Conversion Analytics
- Open your browser's Developer Tools Console on your landing page and type:
  ```javascript
  console.table(JSON.parse(localStorage.getItem('wt_clicks') || '[]'));
  ```
- Check which `utm_content` and `page` generated the most referral clicks.

### Days 17–25: Repurpose Top Performing Pin Hooks
- Take the 3 top-performing pin headlines from Days 1–15 and test them across the other visual templates (e.g. if Template A won for Pokémon, test Template B and Template E with the same copy).
- Publish 1–2 pins daily using Pins 07, 08, and refreshed visual variations.

### Days 26–30: The Evergreen Compounding Engine
- Pin traffic compounds over 30 to 90 days. As Pinterest indexes your keyword-rich boards, pins posted in Week 1 will begin ranking at the top of Pinterest Visual Search for high-intent queries like *"where to buy vintage pokemon cards online"* and *"live sneaker auctions"*.

## 💡 5 Golden Rules for Maximum Clicks ($0 Spend)

1. **Always Use 2:3 Vertical Ratio (1000 × 1500 px):** Horizontal or square images receive up to 60% less screen real estate in the Pinterest mobile feed.
2. **High-Contrast Text Overlays:** 85%+ of Pinterest users browse on mobile devices with high brightness. Big, readable text stops the scroll.
3. **Never Link Directly to Bare Affiliate URLs from Pinterest:** Pinterest often suppresses direct raw affiliate links. Sending traffic to your editorial landing page (`yourusername.github.io/category/`) builds trust, increases conversion rates, and keeps your Pinterest account in 100% good standing.
4. **Maintain Consistent Daily Pinning:** 1–2 pins every day is vastly superior to posting 15 pins in one hour and disappearing for a week.
5. **Keep Disclosures Honest & Clear:** Trust converts. Authentic discovery copy creates lifelong active users who sign up through your link.

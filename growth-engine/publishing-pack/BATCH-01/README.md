# 📦 Pinterest Publishing Pack — BATCH-01

Batch #01 (Pins 01–30): Launch Core

---

## ⚡ 60-Second Manual Upload Workflow

1. Open [pinterest.com/pin-builder/](https://www.pinterest.com/pin-builder/) in your browser.
2. Drag & drop the corresponding graphic from `images/`.
3. Open `titles.txt` & copy the Title.
4. Open `descriptions.txt` & copy the Description.
5. Open `urls.txt` & copy the Destination URL.
6. Select the assigned Board from `schedule.csv`.
7. Click **Publish** (or choose **Schedule** for the recommended date/time).
8. Done!

---

## 📁 Pack Contents
- `images/` — 1000 × 1500 px high-res PNG graphics
- `manifest.csv` — Full metadata table
- `schedule.csv` — Staggered calendar timeline
- `titles.txt` — Quick copy-paste titles
- `descriptions.txt` — Quick copy-paste descriptions with hashtags
- `urls.txt` — Destination links with unique UTM parameters

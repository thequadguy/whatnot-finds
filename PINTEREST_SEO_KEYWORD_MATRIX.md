## 3. The 3-Part Pin Description Formula (High Click-Through Rate)

Every high-converting Pinterest pin description should follow this 3-sentence structure:

1. **The Keyword Hook (Sentence 1):** Answer what the user searched for with natural keyword placement.
   > *Example:* "Looking for authentic vintage Pokémon cards, PSA slabs, and raw singles?"
2. **The Curiosity & Benefit (Sentence 2):** Explain the unique discovery experience.
   > *Example:* "Watch verified streamers unbox packs on 4K camera and bid in fast-paced real-time auctions."
3. **The Clear Call to Action (Sentence 3 + Hashtags):** Tell the user exactly what to do next.
   > *Example:* "Tap through to explore ongoing live Pokémon streams and claim your free signup invite! #pokemontcg #vintagepokemon #pokemoncards"
